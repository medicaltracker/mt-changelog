## Released 1 October 2026  

### :sneezing_face: New automated allergy email notification

- Staff can now choose to receive an automated notification when an allergy has been recorded. This is particularly useful for keeping school allergy champions in the loop.
- The notification is triggered when an illness is recorded, and allergy is selected from the dropdown menu, as in the example below.
- Learn how to control the notifications staff members receive in our [knowledge base article](https://help.medicaltracker.co.uk/en/how-to-set-staff-notifications1).

### :bar_chart: Trust report: What happened next
<img width="2048" height="522" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2427725c-3e0d-49b4-9a80-5dd085ff93e2" />

- From the Reports module, or the Went to hospital tile on the overview, trust users will be able to view a breakdown of incidents at your schools by what happened next. This shows whether the person stayed at school, went home or went to hospital.
- When you drill down to the school level, you’ll be able to see the **RIDDOR** status as well as the status of any associated accident investigation.
- Learn more in our [knowledge base article](https://help.medicaltracker.co.uk/en/trust-reporting-what-happened-next).
